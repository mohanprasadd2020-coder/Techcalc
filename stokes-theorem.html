Stokes' Theorem Statement

If S is an open surface bounded by a closed curve C, and F⃗ is a vector field with continuous partial derivatives:

The Main Formula
$$\oint_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_S (\nabla \times \vec{F}) \cdot \hat{n}\, dS$$
Line Integral around boundary C = Surface Integral of Curl over S
Curl Formula
$$\nabla \times \vec{F} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ \partial_x & \partial_y & \partial_z \\ P & Q & R \end{vmatrix}$$
Curl Components
\((\nabla \times \vec{F})_x = \frac{\partial R}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial Q}{\partial z}\)
\((\nabla \times \vec{F})_y = \frac{\partial P}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial R}{\partial x}\)
\((\nabla \times \vec{F})_z = \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\)
Key Symbols
∇ × F⃗ = Curl (rotation)
n̂ = Unit normal to S
C = CCW boundary curve
